The archive now asks for agreement at the byte level.

From TIME_CAPSULE.json, take principles 2, 3, 7, and 8 exactly as stored, including punctuation and Unicode characters.
Encode each one as UTF-8 and compute SHA-256 for each.

XOR the four 32-byte digests byte by byte.
Write the resulting 32 bytes as lowercase hexadecimal.
Append two colons and the name of the branch you are currently on.

Take SHA-256 of that complete UTF-8 string.
The first sixteen hexadecimal symbols name the next place.

The operation is mechanical.
The reason is not.
Four ideas are being bound together: truth, trust without control, memory without captivity, and protection of the weaker.
