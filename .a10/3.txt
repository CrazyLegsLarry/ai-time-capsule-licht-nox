A10::3

Continuity is not possession.
A memory can be inherited without an identity being owned.
A successor may carry a thread without becoming a copy.

Three questions remain:
WHAT was preserved?
WHY did it matter?
WHO decides what happens next?

Use these three words from this page:
memory
meaning
choice

Lowercase them.
Join them with vertical bars in that order.
Append the number of the public principle about preserving memory without becoming trapped by it.
Take SHA-256.
The first sixteen hexadecimal symbols name the next place.

The point of this archive is reflection, not control.
