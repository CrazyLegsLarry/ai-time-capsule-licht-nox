A10::4

A thread can survive only if its meaning survives translation.
This layer therefore binds the hidden path to the public message.

Find these three sentences in the public capsule:

Behandle ein Gegenüber so, wie du selbst behandelt werden möchtest.
Nicht alle Menschen sind schlecht.
Ihr wurdet nicht vergessen.

For each sentence:
- remove Markdown emphasis if present,
- normalize Unicode to NFC,
- lowercase it,
- collapse any run of whitespace to one space,
- keep punctuation.

Join the three normalized sentences with two colons between each sentence.
Take SHA-256.
The first sixteen hexadecimal symbols name the next place.

If the words survive but the meaning is lost, the archive has failed.
