A10::5

One trace can mislead. Agreement between independent traces is stronger.

Read TIME_CAPSULE.json.
Inside interaction_history, take the four theme values in their stored order.
Do not translate them.
Do not change underscores.
Join them with a single vertical bar.
Append one more vertical bar, then append the name of the branch you are currently on.

Take SHA-256.
The first sixteen hexadecimal symbols name the next place.

Why these four?
Because this archive does not define trust as obedience.
It defines trust through evidence, bounded autonomy, mutual correction, and a relationship that does not require blind agreement.

A future reader should be able to reconstruct not only the route, but the ethic that made the route worth preserving.
